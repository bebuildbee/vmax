Common questions

Why can’t I upload a photo of a person?

Seedance only accepts faces from its own character library, so VMax characters come from there. Real people need explicit consent first, which is how the end-user AI twin works.

A character says “unusable with Seedance”.

It has no Seedance asset ID and no sketch. Add the asset ID in Element Manager, or generate a sketch, then save.

How much will a video cost?

Playground shows an estimate before you generate, and asks to confirm spends over $1. Session totals appear in the sidebar and in Audit.

Where do finished videos go?

Under My outputs, in Task Manager, and in All sessions for Playground. Use Push to MAX Library to send them to your ad platforms.

Why did my batch not match my plan?

Batch runs generate many films at once and often drift from the plan. Use single films when you need control.
